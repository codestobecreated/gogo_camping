Destination · Jawadhu

Jawadhu Hills, a treat for the eyes.

Forest roads, dark skies – and after the monsoon, a sky full of butterflies on their way across the Eastern Ghats.

As featured in The New Indian Express · 29 Aug 2020 “Jawadhu hills, a treat for eyes!” Butterflies swarm the Jawadhu Hills after the monsoon – read the full report. Read article

The Jawadhu Hills are a quiet stretch of the Eastern Ghats in northern Tamil Nadu, spread across Tiruvannamalai and Vellore districts. Forest roads, cool air, small Malayali villages and some of the darkest night skies within a day's drive of Chennai and Bangalore – the Vainu Bappu Observatory at Kavalur sits on these hills for a reason.

A treat for the eyes after the rains

Every year after the monsoon the hills fill up with butterflies. As reported by The New Indian Express (Chennai, 29 August 2020), butterflies migrate from the Western Ghats to the Eastern Ghats in search of damp places and flowering plants, and the Jawadhu Hills are one of their stops.

  • Around 87 types of butterflies are normally spotted in the hills.
  • Look for families such as the Blues, Tigers, Crows, Common Banded Peacock and Lime Butterfly.
  • The flowering season is the best time to see them – they gather in large colonies on the slopes.

go slow on the forest roads – you'll see more wings.

When you're here

Spend the morning on a trail, the afternoon by the water and the night looking up. Ask the GOGO team which trails are best for butterflies on the day you visit.
