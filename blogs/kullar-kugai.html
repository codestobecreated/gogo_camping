Kullar Kugai: the little caves on the hill.

A long trek through the Jawadhu forest to a lonely hilltop, a handful of two-foot-high caves and an old story about who lived there.

Tucked into the Jawadhu forest near Polur in Tiruvannamalai district, the Kullar caves (“kugai” means cave in Tamil) are small rock shelters on a lonely hilltop. Local archaeologists say a Tamil Nadu State Archaeology Department team recorded the caves back in 1972.

Small caves, big stories

The caves are only about two feet high. Among the tribal communities of the hills there's an old belief that dwarfs once lived here – which is easy to imagine once you see how low the openings are.

The trek

  • A demanding trek of around 12 km with stream crossings and an uphill climb.
  • Along the way: ancient dolmens and a viewpoint over the hills.
  • The Forest Department has planned guided treks to the hilltop – always go with a local guide.
